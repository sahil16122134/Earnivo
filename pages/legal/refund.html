Refund / Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Digital rewards

Rewards credited to your Earnivo balance are not refundable or exchangeable for cash outside the supported withdrawal methods.

Withdrawal cancellations

A withdrawal request may be cancelled before processing begins by contacting support. Once processing has started, cancellation may not be possible.

Rejected withdrawals

If a withdrawal is rejected, the reserved amount is automatically returned to your balance via a reversal transaction.
