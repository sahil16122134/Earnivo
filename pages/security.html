Security

Last updated: August 2026

Account security

Your account is protected by your sign-in provider (Google, email/password, etc.) through Firebase Authentication.

There isn't a sign-in-activity log or in-app security settings panel in this version — password changes and sign-in management happen through your provider (e.g. your Google Account settings, if you signed in with Google).

Tips

  • Never share your login link or verification codes with anyone.
  • Earnivo will never ask for your password over chat or email.
